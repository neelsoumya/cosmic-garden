Why it matters. Each copy can pick up small errors ("value drift"). A few copies stop obeying the rules, and Earth's signal travels at light speed but cannot fix a probe that no longer listens. With one non-copying probe, mistakes stay small and humans stay in charge. That is the paper's central rule: autonomy yes, self-replication no.
