Rewilding Natural Intelligence: The Case for a Conservative Approach in Introducing AI into Space

Soumya Banerjee, University of York

The central thesis

Space is a place where errors are irreversible and communication is slow, so the AI we send there should be designed for humility, stewardship and minimal impact. We argue for a categorical ban on autonomous self-replication, while allowing tightly bounded self-repair, with human oversight of high-consequence actions, a strict duty to do no harm and not interfere with extraterrestrial environments, and institutional safeguards such as mission certification and an international Space AI Safety Institute. The aim is for the intelligences we send beyond Earth to act as custodians, not unconstrained agents of change.
